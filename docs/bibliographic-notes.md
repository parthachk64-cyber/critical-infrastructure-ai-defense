**Title discrepancy.** The English title and abstract concern protecting U.S. critical infrastructure from cyberattacks. The publisher page also displays a
German subtitle: *"Nutzung von Quantenkryptographie und künstlicher Intelligenz für die Zahlungssicherheit der nächsten Generation: Eine umfassende Analyse von
Bedrohungen und Gegenmaßnahmen in verteilten Ledger-Umgebungen"* (quantum cryptography, AI and next-generation payment security in distributed-ledger
environments). It matches, almost word for word in translation, the title of a cited reference (Agrawal S. 2024, *Int. J. Sci. Res.* 13(3):682-687), so it is
probably a publisher metadata error. This project uses the English title and infers **no** payment-security, quantum or ledger architecture from the German line.

**Contributions as published.** "N.D. conceptualized the research framework and led the writing of the manuscript. H.K. and K.B.S. contributed to the literature
review and data collection. S.N.H. and J.K. performed technical analysis and contributed to the development of the AI-based detection model. P.C. and H.R. worked on
visualization, figure preparation, and formatting. A.S.A. and R.H. provided supervision, final critical revisions, and contributed to the legal-policy framework
integration." Partha Chakraborty's published contribution is therefore visualization, figure preparation and formatting (with H. Rahman). His role in this repository
is a different one: designated maintainer of an independent companion. Neither implies that he developed the article's detection model.
