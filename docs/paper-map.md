## Correction to the starting assumptions

The brief's figure descriptions were checked against the public images. Two did not match:

* **Fig. 1** is a generic research-design flow (survey + literature review -> analysis -> triangulation -> ethics). It does **not** show
  a Colonial Pipeline source, datasets, policy documents or an expert-validation stage. No expert validation is claimed anywhere in the public
  material and none is asserted here.
* **Fig. 2** is a single-series bar chart of percentages by attack category (IoT, ransomware, malware, cryptojacking, intrusion
  attempts, encrypted threats). It is **not** an incident timeline. No timeline is built from it.

## Genuine reproduction gaps

1. The full text (to confirm datasets, splits, metrics, model settings, units, hardware).
2. Dataset identifiers and versions, schema, class labels, preprocessing, train/test membership.
3. Model specification or checkpoint for the "proposed solution" and the "state of art" series; the Hybrid CNN-LSTM.
4. Score files or per-record predictions (needed to recompute any metric, and to check the 1.0 bars in Fig. 6).
5. Timing conditions for Fig. 4 (what was timed, job size, hardware, software).
6. Source and denominator of the percentages in Fig. 2; the survey instrument and responses behind Fig. 1.
7. Whether the "state of art" series are original measurements or values quoted from cited studies.

See [`data-card.md`](data-card.md) for the minimum artifact list. Without these, direct reproduction of Figs. 2-6 is not possible and this repository does not pretend otherwise.
